Fix 1 — Tile direction pill

Every tile carries a lower is better / higher is better pill between the value and the chart. The "improved 4.3%" verdict at the bottom already encodes good-direction with green / red color, and the sparkline fill matches. The pill is the 4th encoder of the same information. Three after-options below — all preserve the verb-led "improved / worsened" wording (the team's existing decision).

Now — pill always shown
Three real metrics: improved-trend (Violent Crime), worsened-but-still-good-tier (Primary Care Physicians), worsened-and-bad-tier (Renter Cost Burden).
