Current rank: #50 of 50 states (40.6¢, 2025)

Hawaiʻi has ranked #50 since the mid-1990s. The 1973 oil embargo opened a permanent gap: mainland grids shifted to gas and coal while Hawaiʻi stayed on oil. Renewable generation has grown to over 40 percent by 2025, but transition capital costs pass through to ratepayers and the price gap has widened, not closed.
