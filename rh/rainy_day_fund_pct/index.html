Rainy Day Fund

Current rank: #22 of 50 states (14.5%, 2025)

Hawaiʻi ranked near the bottom of states for most of 25 years, with the fund empty in 2003 and below 2 percent through the 2010s. COVID drained it to 0.7 percent. Tourism revenue recovery drove a rapid climb to 14.5 percent by 2025, crossing the 10 percent GFOA-recommended floor for the first time and edging slightly better than the median of 13 percent.
