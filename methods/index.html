How We Compare

  • "Other State Average" is the simple mean of the 49 other states (excluding Hawaiʻi). DC is excluded from state rankings.
  • All data comes from federal sources that report consistently across all 50 states, so every comparison is apples-to-apples.
  • "Better" and "Worse" are determined by the direction of each metric. For example, lower crime rates and lower unemployment are better, while higher graduation rates and higher incomes are better.
  • Rankings run from #1 (best-performing state for that metric) to #50 (worst-performing).
