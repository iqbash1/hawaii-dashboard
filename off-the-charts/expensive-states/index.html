Expensive states are rich. Not Hawaiʻi.

The five most unaffordable states in America measured by years of income needed to buy a home: Hawaiʻi (8.7), California (7.6), Washington (6.1), Colorado (5.9), Massachusetts (5.8).

Four of the five have high incomes to help with the cost. Hawaiʻi is the outlier: highest housing burden in the country, with real per capita income of just $52,272, fifth-from-bottom nationally.

“High-cost peer” comparisons flatter us. We’re the only state in America where the cost has decoupled from the wage entirely.
