Off the Charts · 28 April 2026

Hawaiʻi quadrupled its renewables. The energy prices got worse.

In 2003, Hawaiʻi’s residential electricity price was 16.7¢ per kWh, compared with a national median of 8.1¢. The gap was 8.7¢.

By 2024, Hawaiʻi was at 42.9¢ per kWh, while the national median was 14.8¢. The gap was now 28.0¢.

Over the same two decades, Hawaiʻi quadrupled its renewable share and the energy price gap with the mainland more than tripled.
