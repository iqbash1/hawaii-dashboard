Hawaii Unemployment Rate

Hawaii's unemployment rate was 2.3% in 2025, the second-lowest in the nation (50-state rank: #2, only South Dakota lower at 2.1%). The US median was 4.0%.

Why this matters

Unemployment shows how many people who want work still cannot find it. Hawaii has about 660,000 people in the labor force, so each percentage point on this rate represents roughly 6,600 workers actively seeking jobs. The low headline rate hides a concentration problem: Hawaii's jobs are concentrated in tourism, government, and low-wage services, keeping labor productivity at #46 and wages below the cost of living. The BLS U-6 broader underutilization rate was also low in March 2026 (5.7% vs 8.0% nationally), so the headline number is not hiding slack.
